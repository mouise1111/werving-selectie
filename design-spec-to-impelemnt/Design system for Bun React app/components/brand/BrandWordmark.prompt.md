Type-only brand wordmark — use it wherever a logo would go, in headers, footers and the dark newsletter band.

```jsx
<BrandWordmark name="Werving & Selectie" size="md" />
<BrandWordmark tone="onDark" size="sm" />
```

No logo asset exists in this system. Do not substitute a drawn mark; `showMark={false}` gives plain type when the lime block would clash.
